Wandar is an integrated sales platform for safari operators. Its Sales Agent engages website and WhatsApp prospects 24/7, asks discovery questions, answers guidance questions using your approved safari knowledge, gathers key trip details and keeps conversations moving until human expertise and judgment are needed.

Wandar also surfaces active safari-planning conversations, tracks relevant social mentions, helps your team manage sales activity and next steps, and lets them find answers across rates, itineraries, camp details, quotes and booking history.

Safari demand is easy to miss, and interested travelers can lose momentum when they are sent to a static enquiry form or left waiting on WhatsApp. Wandar surfaces relevant demand, engages website and WhatsApp prospects while their interest is active and keeps sales conversations moving.

It also shows teams what needs action, brings in a safari expert when human judgment is required and helps them find answers quickly across rates, itineraries, camp details, quotes and booking history. Together, this helps operators reach more travelers, run stronger sales conversations and keep more promising enquiries moving toward a booking.

No. It handles the repeatable parts of early-stage sales 24/7 by engaging prospects, asking discovery questions, answering guidance questions using your approved safari knowledge, addressing common concerns and helping travelers understand the value of your safari offering.

It gathers key trip details, keeps conversations moving and brings in your team whenever a traveler needs recommendations, itinerary design, pricing or other input that requires human expertise and judgment.

The Sales Agent works from the safari information you approve, including your website, itineraries, rates, camp and lodge details, policies and sales materials. It uses that information to answer guidance questions, explain your offering and address common traveler concerns.

When it cannot answer confidently or the traveler needs human expertise and judgment, it brings in your team with the conversation context already captured.

It does not replace specialist booking, live availability or itinerary-design systems.
