Out-of-the-box safari sales platform

Built for the complexity of safari sales.

An African safari is expensive, complex and often a once-in-a-lifetime purchase. Travelers are not only comparing itineraries or prices.

They are choosing which operator they trust to understand them, manage concerns and guide them through a decision that can involve thousands of dollars and months of planning.

So competition is not the only obstacle to winning that booking. The work required to sell a safari is demanding too.

Operators are qualifying enquiries, checking rates, researching properties, following up and piecing together information scattered across the business.

Every hour spent on that work is time taken from understanding the traveler, building the relationship and applying the expertise that gets the operator the booking.
